# Tangshan Style · Speculative Essay Engine

[简体中文](./README.md)

Turn a future-facing idea into a speculative essay that people keep reading.

## What It Does

Tangshan Style writes one kind of piece: speculative essays about the future. A technology trend pushed to its limit, an industry reshaped by AI, an extreme assumption becoming real. It does not generate the opinion for you and it does not write tutorials. It turns a future image into prose with historical depth, momentum, and sentences that can carry weight.

## Why Use It

Speculative writing often fails in two ways. One failure is dry abstraction: the piece reasons in the air, but the reader never feels a concrete moment of pressure. The other is inflated language: large claims, heavy adjectives, and grand tone, with little real mechanism underneath.

Tangshan Style solves this by bringing new problems back into older stories and adjacent industries. A new problem is cold and abstract; an old story is warm and already emotionally charged. The essay first gives the reader a sharp, concrete moment, then uses that charge to carry the argument forward.

Its core moves are:

- **Find the counterintuitive angle**: do not follow the obvious reading. Find the cut that changes how the reader sees the topic.
- **Make the structure hurt before it explains**: start from a concrete scene, ignite it with history or another industry, then unfold the future.
- **Forge each sentence**: compression, concrete objects, hard verbs, coined terms with definitions, and numerical contrast make each paragraph worth reading.
- **Keep technical ground under the essay**: a speculative piece can jump forward, but it must launch from real mechanisms, data, or trends.

In one line: Tangshan Style is a **prose engine for speculative futures**. It makes an imagined world feel historically grounded and readable line by line.
