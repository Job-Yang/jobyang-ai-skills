# Cuihuo · Opinion-Hardening Engine

[简体中文](./README.md)

Turn a technical opinion into a piece that can stand in front of peers.

## What It Does

Cuihuo is for one job: you have a technical judgment, opinion, or idea, and you want to write it clearly and firmly enough for other practitioners to challenge it. It is not a tutorial engine and not a general explanation tool. Its job is to make one opinion stand.

## Why Use It

Technical opinion writing usually fails in two ways. One failure is softness: the piece circles around the point, avoids taking a position, and leaves readers unsure what you actually believe. The other is synthetic polish: parallel phrasing, dramatic punctuation, forced triads, and inflated endings that look impressive but feel fake to experienced readers.

Cuihuo pushes against both. Its core belief is simple: **respect comes from the idea; craft is only the carrier.** Every rule serves the same purpose: make what you say matter more than how polished it looks.

- **Make a judgment**: a piece needs at least one claim that can be argued with. Covering every side usually means saying nothing.
- **One section, one idea**: explain it and move on. Repeating the same point in different clothes is the fastest way to lose technical readers.
- **Say precise things precisely**: do not lower precise concepts into metaphors. Use a metaphor only when it genuinely explains something hard.
- **Use words that fit the object**: strong wording is not enough. A verb or adjective must match what it describes.
- **Keep citations readable**: keep short clickable markers in the body and move full source titles to the reference list.
- **Visualize strong contrasts**: when the argument depends on a sharp numerical gap, suggest an infographic so the reader can see the gap before reading the prose.
- **Stay level**: do not perform superiority. Present observations, mechanisms, and evidence; let the reader decide that the point holds.

One-line rule: **the opinion matters more than the prose; be professional, restrained, direct, and clear.**
